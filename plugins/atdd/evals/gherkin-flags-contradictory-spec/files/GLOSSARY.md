# Glossary

- **Borrower**: a person registered with the library. Never "user", "member" or "patron" in domain language.
- **Library card**: a borrower's proof of registration. A staff library card belongs to a library employee.
- **Item**: anything that can be borrowed: a book, a film, a board game.
- **Loan**: one item borrowed by one borrower, with a due date.
- **Due date**: the last day of a loan, in the home branch's time zone.
- **Home branch**: the branch a borrower is registered at. Every branch has one IANA time zone.
- **Overdue notice**: an email telling a borrower that a loan is past its due date.
- **Return**: handing an item back at any branch, which ends the loan.
