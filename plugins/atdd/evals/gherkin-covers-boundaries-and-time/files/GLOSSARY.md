# Glossary

- **Member**: a person with an account in the app. Never "user" or "customer" in domain language.
- **Tracker**: the wearable device or phone that counts a member's steps.
- **Step count**: one batch of steps for a time interval, synced from the member's tracker.
- **Daily steps**: the sum of a member's step counts for one calendar day.
- **Calendar day**: midnight to midnight in the member's time zone.
- **Step goal**: the number of daily steps a member aims for.
- **Celebration**: an in-app message congratulating a member on reaching their step goal.
- **Workout**: a recorded session of exercise with a start time, an end time and a sport.
