# Requirements: FIT-31 Daily step goal celebrations

Source: .scratch/step-goals/spec.md

- R1: The step goal is a whole number of steps from 1,000 to 50,000; other values are rejected.
  > Spec: "The step goal is a whole number of steps from 1,000 to 50,000."
- R2: A member is celebrated when their daily steps go over the step goal.
  > Spec: "When the member's daily steps reach the goal, the app shows the member one celebration for that day."
- R3: A member gets at most one celebration per calendar day.
  > Spec: "A member gets at most one celebration per calendar day, however many more steps they take."
- R4: Pausing celebrations stops them; the goal is kept.
  > Spec: "Pausing celebrations stops them immediately. The goal is kept"
