# Requirements: a02-celebrations A02. Goal celebrations

Source: docs/specs/a02-celebrations.md

## Requirements

- **R1**: When a member's synced steps for a day reach their daily step goal, the member gets the push message "Goal reached: <steps> steps!", with the synced steps written with thousands separators.
  > Spec: "When a member's steps for a day sync and reach their daily step goal, the member gets a celebration: a push message "Goal reached: <steps> steps!""
- **R2**: When a member's synced steps for a day stay below their daily step goal, the member gets no push message.
  > Spec: "Steps below the goal get no push message."

## Out of scope

- Weekly totals.
